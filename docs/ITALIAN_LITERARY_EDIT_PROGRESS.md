## Important rule

Short paragraphs or one-word lines are **not automatically errors**. They are retained when they serve a deliberate suspense, sound, visual, or emotional beat (for example, "Toc. Toc. Toc." or isolated time/number beats). Ordinary accidental fragmentation is merged into normal literary paragraphs.
