## Next distribution channels

### 1. Apple Books
Direct Apple Books publishing is supported through Apple Books Publishing Portal / iTunes Connect. Apple accepts EPUB and requires EPUBCheck validation for submitted books.

Status: **0% — next channel**

### 2. Google Play Books
Google Play Books supports ebook sales in Italy. The Partner Center accepts EPUB/PDF and allows publishers/authors to manage territories, DRM and list prices.

Status: **0%**

### 3. Kobo Writing Life
Kobo Writing Life is a direct self-publishing channel for ebooks and supports worldwide territorial rights. The current Kobo terms describe a 70% Independent Publisher Program rate when its pricing and territorial criteria are met.

Status: **0%**

### 4. StreetLib
StreetLib can distribute ebooks to multiple stores and channels, including Kobo, Apple Books and Google Play. It also provides Italian national distribution options.

Because this edition is already being published directly through Amazon KDP, **do not enable StreetLib's Amazon distribution for this same edition**. StreetLib explicitly advises excluding its Amazon channel when the book is already published or intended to be self-published through KDP.

Status: **0%**

## Distribution rule

This edition is not enrolled in KDP Select, so the project can evaluate non-Amazon ebook channels. Before every additional upload, verify that:
- the same Italian edition is not accidentally enrolled in an exclusive program;
- metadata, author name, title and cover remain consistent;
- pricing and territorial rights are intentional;
- the distributor is not creating a duplicate Amazon edition.

## Recommended work sequence

1. Wait for Amazon KDP review result.
2. Prepare Apple Books package and submit.
3. Prepare Google Play Books package and submit.
4. Prepare Kobo Writing Life package and submit.
5. Evaluate StreetLib for Italian retail/library reach, excluding Amazon distribution.
6. Record each store's URL, publication status, price and publication date in the project checkpoint.
